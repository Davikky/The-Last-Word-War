# Purpose

This document summarizes the intellectual property principles governing The Last Word War™ project.

---

# Copyright

Copyright applies to:

* Source code
* Documentation
* Rulebooks
* Research papers
* Artwork
* Website content
* Educational materials

Copyright ownership remains with the human creators and contributors.

---

# Trademark

The project name:

The Last Word War™

is regarded as a trademark asset.

Future registration may be pursued as the project evolves.

---

# Publications

Academic publications shall acknowledge:

* Human contributors
* Supporting technologies

Artificial intelligence tools shall not be listed as authors.

Human authors remain responsible for:

* Ideas
* Analysis
* Interpretation
* Conclusions

---

# Human Contribution Principle

The project seeks to preserve:

2/3 Human Contribution

1/3 AI Assistance

This principle applies to:

* Software development
* Documentation
* Publications
* Educational materials

---

# Artificial Intelligence Policy

Artificial intelligence may assist with:

* Coding support
* Documentation drafting
* Research assistance
* Architecture reviews

Artificial intelligence may not:

* Claim ownership
* Claim authorship
* Direct project governance
* Replace human contributors

---

# Long-Term Objective

The Last Word War™ seeks to demonstrate that artificial intelligence can strengthen human collaboration while preserving human agency, authorship, and meaning.
