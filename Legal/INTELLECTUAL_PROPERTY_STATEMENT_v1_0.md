# Purpose

This document summarizes the intellectual property principles governing The Last Word War™ and associated research, publications, documentation, educational materials, and software assets.

The document is intended to establish a clear framework for stewardship, authorship, attribution, and future intellectual property management.

---

# Project Stewardship

The Last Word War™ is stewarded by Victor A. Adeyemi under the Da-aim Services research and development ecosystem.

Project stewardship includes:

* Project direction
* Governance
* Documentation oversight
* Research development
* Publication coordination
* Long-term preservation of project philosophy

# Trademark

The project name:

The Last Word War™

is regarded as a trademark asset of the project ecosystem.

The project may pursue formal trademark registration in appropriate jurisdictions as development, adoption, or commercial activity increases.

Trademark protection is intended to preserve project identity and integrity rather than restrict academic discussion or research.

# Publications

Academic publications associated with The Last Word War™ shall acknowledge:

* Human contributors
* Supporting technologies where appropriate

Artificial intelligence tools shall not be listed as academic authors.

Human authors remain responsible for:

* Ideas
* Analysis
* Interpretation
* Conclusions
* Final approval

Authorship remains a human responsibility.

---

# Human Contribution Principle

The project seeks to preserve:

2/3 Human Contribution

1/3 AI Assistance

This principle serves as a philosophical guideline for:

* Software development
* Documentation
* Research
* Publications
* Educational materials

The principle is intended to preserve human agency, creativity, authorship, and stewardship.

---

# Artificial Intelligence Policy

Artificial intelligence may assist with:

* Coding support
* Documentation drafting
* Research assistance
* Architecture reviews
* Educational assistance

Artificial intelligence may not:

* Claim ownership
* Claim authorship
* Claim stewardship
* Direct project governance
* Replace human contributors

Artificial intelligence functions solely as an assistant to human contributors.

---

# Future Contributors

Future contributors shall be recognized according to the nature and significance of their contributions.

Contributor recognition may include:

* Authorship
* Acknowledgement
* Documentation records
* Publication credits

Recognition shall remain consistent with the project's authorship policy.

---

# Long-Term Objective

The Last Word War™ seeks to demonstrate that artificial intelligence can strengthen human collaboration while preserving human agency, authorship, stewardship, and meaning.

The project aims to contribute to:

* Human-centered computing
* Educational technology
* Responsible AI
* Collaborative creativity
* Human-centered game design

under the Da-aim Services research and development ecosystem.
