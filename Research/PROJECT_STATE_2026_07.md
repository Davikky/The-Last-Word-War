# Executive Summary

The Last Word War™ has completed its foundational design phase and entered an Architecture Stabilization Phase.

The project's philosophy, gameplay rules, software architecture, and documentation have now been synchronized around a single guiding principle:

> **Human ↔ Human Interaction**

The gameplay experience intentionally preserves human judgment while software provides objective computational coordination through the principle of **Computational Facilitation**.

Current development focuses on completing frontend architectural refactoring, validating gameplay through Playtest #2, implementing the remaining approved gameplay improvements, and preparing the project for its first research publication.

---

# Project Identity

The Last Word War™ is a human-centered multiplayer language game in which players collaboratively construct an evolving chain of ideas by beginning each sentence with the final word of the previous accepted sentence.

The project seeks to encourage:

* conversation;
* creativity;
* reasoning;
* learning;
* collaboration;
* friendly competition.

The project deliberately places Human ↔ Human interaction at the center of the gameplay experience.

Artificial intelligence currently serves only as a development, documentation, and research assistant.

Artificial intelligence is **not** part of official gameplay.

The official gameplay philosophy now distinguishes between:

Future gameplay improvements will primarily involve implementation rather than redesign.
