# UX Feedback Round 1

Date: 2026-06-18

Source:

First external multiplayer playtest

---

## UX-001

Title:

Guest Waiting-State Confusion

Observation:

Guest entered the lobby successfully but did not understand that the Host must start the game.

Recommendation:

Display:

"Waiting for Host to Start Game..."

or

"Waiting for Oasis to Start Game..."

Priority:

High

Status:

Open

---

## UX-002

Title:

Timeout Event Visibility

Observation:

Players may not immediately realize that a timeout occurred.

Recommendation:

Display temporary notification:

"Oasis timed out."

"Penalty applied."

"Turn passes to Davikky."

Priority:

Medium

Status:

Open

---

## UX-003

Title:

Countdown Warning

Observation:

Players receive no visual warning before timeout.

Recommendation:

Highlight timer during final 10 seconds.

Priority:

Medium

Status:

Open
