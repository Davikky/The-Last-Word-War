# PLAYTEST_2_PREPARATION

Project: The Last Word War

Version: v0.1.1

Date: 2026-06-19

Status: Ready

---

# Purpose

Playtest #2 is intended to evaluate how new players interact with The Last Word War with minimal guidance.

The primary objective is not to test new features, but to observe human behavior, usability, and overall enjoyment.

---

# Test Participants

Target:

2–4 first-time players

Preferred:

Players with no prior exposure to the game.

---

# Test Conditions

The tester should avoid providing excessive explanations.

Players should be encouraged to:

* Create or join a room
* Read the landing page
* Explore the interface naturally
* Ask questions only when necessary

Observations should be recorded.

---

# Primary Objectives

1. Validate onboarding experience

Can players understand the game from the landing page and interface alone?

---

2. Validate lobby experience

Can players understand:

* Room creation
* Room joining
* Host responsibilities
* Match start procedure

---

3. Validate gameplay awareness

Can players understand:

* Whose turn it is
* Current word requirements
* Timeout penalties
* Score progression

---

4. Validate game completion

Can players understand:

* Final Round
* Sudden Death
* Draw Resolution
* Inactivity Resolution

---

5. Validate feedback collection

Can players locate and use the feedback system after a match?

---

# Success Criteria

The playtest shall be considered successful if:

* Players join without assistance.
* Players understand whose turn it is.
* Players understand the sentence rule.
* Players complete a match.
* Players understand match outcomes.
* At least one player submits feedback.
* At least one player indicates willingness to play again.

---

# Observation Checklist

Record:

* Confusing moments
* Unexpected behavior
* Questions asked by players
* Bugs encountered
* Positive reactions
* Suggestions for improvement

---

# Desired Outcome

The goal of Playtest #2 is to identify usability improvements rather than gameplay mechanics.

The game engine is considered feature-complete for the current alpha phase.

Future development priorities should be guided by player behavior and feedback.
