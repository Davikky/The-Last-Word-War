# Vision

The Last Word War™ is a human-centered multiplayer language game designed to promote meaningful Human ↔ Human interaction through structured conversation.

Players build conversations by beginning each sentence with the final word of the previous sentence.

Technology exists only to facilitate communication, validation, and gameplay.

The project also serves as a research initiative exploring human-centered computing, educational gaming, and AI-assisted creative systems.

# Long-Term Vision

The Last Word War™ seeks to demonstrate how artificial intelligence can support human collaboration without replacing human beings at the center of creative and educational activities.

The project aims to evolve into both:

* A multiplayer conversational platform
* A human-centered AI research initiative

under the Da-aim Services research and development ecosystem.
